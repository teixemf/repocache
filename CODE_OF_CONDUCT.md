# Code of Conduct

Contributors and maintainers are expected to interact respectfully and professionally, discuss technical ideas rather than attacking people, welcome differing experience levels, and protect confidential information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of personal information and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful comments or restrict participation as appropriate.

Use GitHub's abuse reporting tools for conduct concerns. Security vulnerabilities must follow the [private security reporting process](SECURITY.md); do not publish exploit details in public discussions.
